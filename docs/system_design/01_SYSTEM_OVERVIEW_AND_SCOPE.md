# Social Media Booster Agentic System

## Persona
A new content creator is trying to grow an audience under uncertainty, competing advice, algorithm opacity, and emotional fatigue.

## Primary Goal
Implement the “Zero-to-1000 Followers System” to help a new YouTube content creator grow their audience by managing judgment tensions and achieve sustainable growth. The creator has already started creating content and needs to optimize their workflow and engagement strategies. I am focused on the engagement strategies after the content is published that can help the creator in deciding how should they approach their next content.

## Secondary Goal
Make the agentic system visible to the users. While the primary goal of the product is to help the content creator, a secondary goal here is to also make the agentic system visible to the users since this work is being done for an AI prototyping grad course. I want to be able to see how different agents are responding to a given query.

## Scope Boundary
The system operates **exclusively in the post-publish space**. It assumes content has already been created and posted by the creator. It does not participate in ideation, drafting, scheduling, or publishing.

- **Input:** A piece of published content + its performance data + audience reactions.
- **Output:** Analysis, patterns, and skill-building provocations.

This boundary is deliberate. By constraining the system to the reflective space after a post is live, we ensure every agent is oriented toward understanding and learning, not production and optimization.
